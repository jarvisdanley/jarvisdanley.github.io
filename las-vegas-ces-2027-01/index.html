🚝 Getting Around

MY PICK
① Free CES shuttle from MGM Grand
Board at the Grand Garden Entrance, a badge is required. Hours: Tue 8 AM–6 PM, Wed–Fri 7:30 AM–6:30 PM. It runs to LVCC and the Venetian, and the Tech Express links those two all day.
CES transportation flyer
② Las Vegas Monorail · MGM Grand → LVCC
The station is inside MGM; take the escalators by the Bell Desk down to the Underground. It's ~10 min to LVCC and beats Strip traffic. $5.75 a ride, or $30.85 for 3 days (eTicket). Hours: Tue–Thu 7 AM–2 AM. The Vegas Loop (Teslas in tunnels) is free inside the LVCC campus.

☕ Coffee

Vegas's specialty scene is entirely off-Strip. There's nothing local worth it within walking distance of MGM, so fold one of these into a downtown evening or do a Tuesday-afternoon run after you land. Starbucks is the on-Strip fallback.

TOP PICK
Vesta Coffee Roasters
The roastery that kicked off the Arts District's coffee scene in 2016; the Probat hums in the back. Order a cortado and take home a bag of the Goose Bumps espresso blend.
📍 1114 S Casino Center Blvd · Arts District · ~5.7 mi, 10 min · daily 7 AM–4 PM
ROASTER
Mothership Coffee Roasters · Fergusons
Locally roasted, Latina-owned, in a restored motel courtyard on Fremont East. It pairs with an 11th Street Records stop next door.
📍 1028 E Fremont St · ~6 mi, ~15 min
PublicUs
Coffee bar plus a real breakfast and lunch menu, the best sit-down-and-work option. Order the macadamia nut latte.
📍 1126 E Fremont St · ~6 mi · daily 7 AM–3 PM

🥩 Steak

Vegas is a genuine steak town. You have two dinners (Tue and Wed) and maybe Thu, so book them.

TOP PICK
Golden Steer Steakhouse
Open since 1958, the oldest steakhouse in Vegas, with Sinatra's booth, leather and tableside Caesar. It's the most Vegas dinner you can have that isn't on the Strip. ⚠️ Book direct only: they don't honor third-party reservations, and the whole party must be present to be seated.
📍 308 W Sahara Ave · ~3.5 mi, ~10 min · opens 4:30 PM, last seating 9:45 · 702-384-4470
WALK FROM MGM
Bavette's Steakhouse & Bar · Park MGM
A dark, moody, French-accented Chicago chophouse. It's the no-car night: walk over after the Wednesday floor closes.
📍 Park MGM · ~0.4 mi walk
Bazaar Meat by José Andrés · Palazzo
Open-fire kitchen in a 20,000 sq ft room, with shareable large-format cuts. Pair it with the Tuesday keynote next door. Booking is on SevenRooms, and the bar takes walk-ins.
📍 3325 S Las Vegas Blvd · ~1.4 mi · daily from 5 PM

Also good: Barry's Downtown Prime at Circa (old-school, ~5 mi) · SW Steakhouse at Wynn (lake-show view).

🛡️ Safety & Travel Alerts

  • Labor: the Culinary Union's MGM and Caesars contracts were signed in late 2024 and run for years. The pending MGM sale doesn't reopen them, so there's no strike risk for CES week.
  • Flights: LAS was on the FAA's shutdown flight-cut list; that's ending now that the shutdown is over. ⚠️ The deal only funds the government through Jan 30, so a second shutdown can't hit before your trip. I'll still watch it.
  • Crowds: CES draws ~140k people. Strip traffic at 6 PM is the real hazard, so use the monorail or shuttle instead of Ubers at rush hour.
  • It's a domestic trip, so there's no State Dept advisory. I'll re-sweep every heartbeat.
