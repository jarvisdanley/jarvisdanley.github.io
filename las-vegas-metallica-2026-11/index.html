TOP PICK
🥩 CUT by Wolfgang Puck
Wolfgang Puck's flagship steakhouse — AAA Four Diamond. USDA Prime 35-day dry-aged and A5 wagyu. The 40 oz bone-in tomahawk is the headline.
📍 The Palazzo · venetianlasvegas.com · reserve ahead
🥩 Delmonico Steakhouse
Emeril Lagasse's Vegas institution. Classic steakhouse with a New Orleans accent — Wine Spectator Grand Award winner. Chateaubriand carved tableside.
📍 The Venetian · venetianlasvegas.com
SEAFOOD
🐟 estiatorio Milos
Market-fresh Mediterranean seafood with a signature fish market. Their three-course prix fixe is widely called the best lunch deal on the Strip — the smart play before a show.
📍 The Venetian · venetianlasvegas.com
🥢 Mott 32
The most distinctive table on property — Cantonese, applewood-roasted Peking duck and Iberico pork. Not a steakhouse, but the one dinner that feels like an event.
📍 The Venetian
🥐 Bouchon Bistro
Thomas Keller's French bistro. The breakfast/lunch pick — and Bouchon Bakery next door is the easiest good coffee on property.
📍 The Venetian, 10th floor
OLD VEGAS
🥩 The Golden Steer Steakhouse
The oldest steakhouse in Las Vegas, serving since 1958 — and the best atmosphere in the city for a steak man. Red leather booths named for Sinatra, Elvis and Ali, tuxedoed servers, Caesar salad made tableside, bananas Foster flambéed at the table. Prime aged beef, wet-aged 35 days.
📍 308 W Sahara Ave · ~1 mi from Harrah's · goldensteer.com · reserve early, it books out
