📍 3201 Corrine Dr, Orlando, FL 32803 · lineageroasting.com · Mon–Sat 8a–7p, Sun 8a–6p

🏋️ Gym — Planet Fitness

Nothing within 2 miles of the Embassy Suites. This stretch of US-192 is the same big-box-gym dead zone as the rest of the resort corridor. Nearest club is Planet Fitness, 3107 W Vine St, Kissimmee, FL 34741 — ~3.6 mi straight / ~4.5 mi drive (~12 min), open 24h. Next: 3214 John Young Pkwy (The Loop), ~4.0 mi.
Nearest the conference: Planet Fitness Orlando (Dr. Phillips), 8956 Turkey Lake Rd, Ste 1000, ~7.4 mi from the Swan & Dolphin, open 24h. Not worth the detour on a session day.
Use the hotel gym instead. The Embassy Suites has an on-site fitness center, free to guests — on a Gartner day with a 10 AM keynote and an evening event, a 12-minute each-way drive to a big-box gym isn't worth it. Work out on property.

💡 Local Intel

Two terminals, two time zones in your head: flights below are shown local (CDT out of KC, EDT/EST in Orlando). Orlando runs one hour ahead of Kansas City. All conference times on this page are ET.
Southwest at MCO: Terminal A, gates 100–129, behind the airside train. Arrive 2 hours early; book the free MCO Reserve window to skip security.
The drive: Embassy Suites ↔ Swan & Dolphin is ~20 minutes, mostly toll road (SR-417 / Beachline). Keep the rental's toll pass active or carry a SunPass so you don't wait at cash booths.
Return-day timing: sessions end 1:45 PM and the flight leaves 4:05 PM. That's the whole reason the car must go back by ~2:45 PM — leave the venue right at close, don't linger in the Xpo hall.
Ongoing: 🚫 no alcohol — bars are for atmosphere and food. All temps °F.
